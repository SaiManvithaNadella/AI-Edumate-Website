What We Solve

Students today face information overload and low engagement. Teachers spend hours creating content. AI‑Edumate solves both by delivering generative AI-powered educational experiences.

Personalized Course Generation

Create a full 10-module curriculum in minutes, tailored by grade level, topic, and learning goals.

Microlearning for Modern Attention

Deliver AI-generated short lessons like Instagram Reels to match the average 8.25s adult attention span.

Empathetic AI Tutoring

Students interact with a chatbot tutor that offers support, explanations, and encouragement.
